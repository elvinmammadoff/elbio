Systems_first.

I work where engineering and design overlap. My background is in real-time dashboards, KYC flows, observability tools, and other interfaces where users care more about reliability than personality — and where personality has to be earned anyway.

I write CSS, I read API contracts, I sit in standups. The work I'm proudest of looks effortless because someone, somewhere, sweated the milliseconds.

Previously: design lead at a Series B observability company. Before that: founding designer at two YC startups.
