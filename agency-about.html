We're a small, senior team that treats every project like our own — strategic enough to matter, crafted enough to remember.

Our story

Founded on a simple belief.

Elbio started in 2016 as two designers freelancing out of a shared apartment. We were tired of the agency model where senior people sell the work and juniors deliver it.

Nine years later we're still deliberately small — a team where the people you meet in the pitch are the people who do the work. That's not a constraint; it's the whole point.

We've since shipped 120+ projects across fintech, wellness, e-commerce, and culture — and we're just getting started.

How we work

The principles behind every project.

Strategy first

Beautiful work that doesn't move the business is just decoration. We start with the problem, not the pixels.

Obsessive craft

We sweat the details others skip — the easing curve, the kerning, the empty state. It all adds up.

True partnership

No handoffs, no account managers playing telephone. You work directly with the people making the thing.
