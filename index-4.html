About

A studio of one, by design.

I keep the practice small on purpose. Working with a single client at a time means I can sit with the strategy long enough for it to actually become craft — and bring that care all the way from research to dev handoff.

I work mostly with founders and editors who care about typography, hierarchy, and the difference between fast and right. Past partners include independent magazines, Series A startups, and a few quiet legacy brands rethinking their digital posture.

Off the page: bookbinding, slow film, and an obstinate interest in early-2000s Swiss editorial design.

— Elbio
Practice

What I do, in long-form.

Three practice areas, often overlapping. Engagements run six to sixteen weeks and include strategy, design, and a long enough handoff to leave the work in better hands than I found it.

i.

Brand & Identity

Visual identities for editorial-leaning founders. Logo, type system, color, motion, and guidelines that respect the people who will use them.

Naming Logo Type System Guidelines
ii.

Editorial Web

High-craft websites for magazines, studios, and independent voices. Lenis, GSAP, hand-built CMS templates — the kind of site that ages well.

Site Design Art Direction CMS Templating
iii.

Product Design

End-to-end product design for founders who haven't quite hired in yet. I sit with the strategy long enough for the UI to inherit it.

UX Strategy UI Design Design QA
