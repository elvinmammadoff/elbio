About me

I design with intention, not decoration.

My work sits at the intersection of systematic thinking and visual wit. I've shipped products for Series A startups and Fortune 500s — and I bring the same obsessive care to both.

Before going independent, I led design at two Y Combinator-backed companies and spent three years at a boutique branding studio in Berlin. That experience gave me both the strategic vocabulary of product and the craft vocabulary of brand.

Right now I'm particularly interested in: editorial interfaces, design systems that scale without losing soul, and products that genuinely respect the user's attention.

— Elbio
