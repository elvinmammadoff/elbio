About

Twelve years behind the lens.

I shoot on a mix of medium-format film and digital, chasing natural light and honest expression. My work has appeared in editorial features, brand campaigns, and gallery shows across three continents.

Whether it's a quiet portrait or a sprawling location shoot, my approach is the same: slow down, pay attention, and let the moment arrive.
