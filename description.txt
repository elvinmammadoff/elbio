<h3 id="item-description__elbio-creative-portfolio-agency-html-template">Elbio – Creative Portfolio &amp; Agency HTML Template</h3>
<p>Welcome to <strong>Elbio</strong>, a premium, motion-rich HTML template for creatives, freelancers, designers, developers, and studios. With <strong>04 distinct homepage demos</strong> (including a full <strong>multipage agency</strong> demo), <strong>15+ pages</strong>, a hand-built <strong>GSAP animation system</strong>, a custom magic cursor, and clean, well-commented code, Elbio gives you everything you need to launch a portfolio that genuinely stands out.</p>

<p><strong>Elbio</strong> is built for people who care about craft. Every interaction — the word-mask hero reveal, the clip-path portrait, the scroll-linked tilt cards, the page-transition wipes — is purposeful, smooth, and performant. No page builders, no bloat: just fast, accessible, framework-free HTML, CSS, and JavaScript you can fully control.</p>
