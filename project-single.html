Case Study

Lumora
SaaS Analytics
Platform

Role
Lead Product Designer
Year

Deliverables
UI Design, Design System, Prototypes
Client
Lumora Inc.
Duration
4 months
The Problem

Marketing teams drowning in data, starving for insight.

Lumora's customers were marketing teams at mid-market B2B companies — 10 to 200 people, managing campaigns across 6–12 channels. The existing dashboard had been patched incrementally over four years. What started as a clean reporting tool had become a maze.

The Solution

Three mental models. One coherent system.

The first two weeks were research-only: 18 user interviews, a competitive audit across 9 analytics tools, and a card sorting exercise with 24 participants to understand how users mentally categorize their work.

The Results

68% to 19% abandonment. Measured, not estimated.

  • 2.3min
    Time-to-insight Down from 14 minutes. Measured via session replay across 340 users.
  • -49%
    Insights page abandonment From 68% to 19%. Users are now completing the analysis flow they start.
  • 4×
    Design system adoption Adopted by 4 additional product teams within 3 months of launch.
