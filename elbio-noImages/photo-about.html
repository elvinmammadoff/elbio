Behind the lens.

I'm Mara Voss — a photographer chasing natural light and honest expression for the past twelve years.

My story

Slow looking, honest frames.

I started with a borrowed film camera and a darkroom in a friend's bathroom. Twelve years later I still shoot a mix of medium-format film and digital — because the constraint of film makes me see better.

My work has appeared in editorial features, brand campaigns, and gallery shows across three continents. But the work I'm proudest of is usually the quietest: a real expression, caught at the right moment.

How I work

What every shoot gets.

Film & digital

A hybrid workflow — the texture and patience of film, the flexibility of digital. You get the best of both.

Natural light first

I build around available light wherever possible. It's softer, more honest, and it flatters real people.

Hand-finished edits

Every selected frame is colour-graded and retouched by hand — never batch-filtered, never outsourced.
